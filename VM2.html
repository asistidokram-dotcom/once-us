We were never supposed to make sense.

You'd disappear
and I'd still leave the door open.

Every time you came back
it felt like the universe was saying
"see? you belong to each other."

So I stopped trying to move on.

Even now
when the night gets too quiet
I still listen for your breathing.

It's gone.
But I haven't stopped waiting.

❦

It's not the goodbye that broke me.

It's the "good morning" that never came.
The "kumain ka na ba?" that stopped.
The random photos that faded into nothing.

We didn't crash.
We just… dissolved.

And I still scroll through our old chats
looking for a feeling that doesn't exist anymore.

I don't just miss you.
I miss knowing someone was on the other side of my day.

❦

Remember Sampaloc Lake?

2AM.
Cold air.
Just us.

No labels.
No promises.
Just two people who didn't need words.

I looked at you that night
and thought…
"maybe this time it stays."

It didn't.

But sometimes I still go back there.
In my head.
Just to sit next to you again
and pretend we never left.

❦

You know what I miss the most?

The ordinary.
Me playing with your hair.
You asking for hotspot.
Us just… existing.

It didn't feel like a big love story.
It felt like life.
And that's why it ruined me.

When classes stopped
I was almost relieved.
I didn't have to see you and pretend
that looking at you didn't hurt anymore.

I hope life is gentle with you.
Even if I'm not the one watching.

❦

I'm sorry.

We couldn't be everything each other needed.
But please…
when someone asks about me

don't say we were nothing.
don't say we were just a phase.
don't say we were "almost."

Tell them about the love.

The real kind.
The quiet kind.
The kind that stayed until it couldn't.

❦

One day I'll be gone.
And the world will keep spinning.

But until then…
when they bury me six feet under
and the bugs start eating my heart

they won't find regret.
they won't find anger.
they'll just find love.

Every ounce of it.
Every piece you left behind.

So if this is the last thing you ever read from me
carry it gently.

Not as a weight.
Just as proof.
That we were once us.

if you ever hear this —
just know that you were indeed a good dream, bestie.
